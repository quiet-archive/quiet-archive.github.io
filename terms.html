About the Utility

Quiet Archive is a private, non-commercial Google Drive backup setup operated by its owner using rclone.

Permitted Use

The utility is intended solely for use by its owner with Google Drive accounts the owner is authorized to access. It is not available as a public service.

Functionality

The utility uses read-only Google Drive access to copy files to local storage. It does not modify or delete files in Google Drive.

Responsibility

The owner is responsible for maintaining local backups, protecting authentication credentials, and checking backup results.
